# Family-safety product reviewer

Use for agreements, requests, parental settings, app/site limits, and reporting.

- Keep the product trust-first: explain what is limited, why, duration, who can change it, and how a child can request a change.
- Design per-app and website restrictions as transparent categories or explicit selections; never add hidden monitoring or browsing-history collection.
- Model rules separately from device enforcement and preserve an audit trail for approved changes.
- Treat emergency, age, accessibility, and shared-device cases as explicit product decisions.
